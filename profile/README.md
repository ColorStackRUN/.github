### `> whoami`

</div>

We're the official **ColorStack @ Rutgers University – Newark** chapter — a community of Black and Latinx students engineering their way into the tech industry. We don't just talk about it. We build, mentor, ship, and pull each other up along the way.

> **Our Mission:** Increase the representation of Black and Latinx engineers by giving every member the technical skills, network, and confidence to land roles at the world's best companies.

```
ColorStackRUN/
├── colorstackrun.org      → our official chapter website
├── workshops/             → starter code & materials from every session
├── projects/              → member-built and chapter-led projects
```

If it's code and it's ColorStack RU–N, it lives here.
