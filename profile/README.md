# ColorStack @ Rutgers University – Newark

Welcome to the official GitHub organization for **ColorStack @ Rutgers University – Newark**.

ColorStack is a national community dedicated to increasing the representation of Black and Latinx students in computing. Our Rutgers–Newark chapter empowers members through technical workshops, mentorship, networking, and hands-on projects that prepare us for impactful careers in tech.

---

## About This Organization

This GitHub organization is the home for everything code-related at ColorStack @ Rutgers–Newark, including:

- Our official chapter website
- Workshop materials and starter code
- Member projects and collaborative builds
- Internal tooling and automation

If it has to do with our chapter and lives in a repository, you'll find it here.
